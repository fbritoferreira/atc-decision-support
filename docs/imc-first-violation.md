# The first instrument-condition violation is one the visual-separation
# explanation does not cover

Status: measured 2026-08-26, superseded in scale but not in substance. The
sample stood at one pair when this was written and holds six across every
window collected since, two of them violating, which is still far short of the
thirty a proportion comparison of this kind wants; `imc-pool.py` recomputes
three of those six on a checkout holding only the surviving dumps. Nothing
below is retracted and the reading has not changed. Added 2026-09-01, because
a note opening on one pair reads as current to anyone who does not check the
date, and this one is cited by both papers.

One pair, so it settles nothing statistically. It
is recorded in full because it is qualitatively the most interesting pair this
project has sampled, and because the way the contrast moved in twenty-four
hours is itself the lesson.

## The direction reversed as soon as the sample grew

Yesterday the pooled instrument-condition sample held one wake pair, legally
separated, against 35 per cent of visual-condition pairs violating. Both papers
reported that as pointing the way the visual-separation hypothesis predicts,
with an explicit caveat that one pair against an expectation of 1.9 cannot be
separated from sampling noise.

Overnight the watcher caught two more instrument-condition windows. The pooled
figures now read:

| condition | pairs | violating | rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| instrument | 3 | 1 | 33% |
| visual | 427 | 149 | 35% |

Expected instrument-condition pairs on the exposure model: 3.2. Observed: 3.
The model continues to be right about how many pairs the sampling should
produce, and the violation rate it produced is now indistinguishable from
visual conditions.

That is what a caveat about sampling noise means when it is honoured rather
than recited. Going from one pair to three moved the reading from zero per cent
to thirty-three, and reversed the direction of the inference. Any conclusion
drawn from the n=1 reading yesterday would have been wrong today. The papers
carried the caveat, so they do not have to withdraw a claim; they do have to
report the new number, which is the same discipline applied to a result that
happens to be less flattering.

## The one violation, and why it is not the same as the others

| field | value |
|---|---|
| leader | UAL838, CWT category B (a heavy) |
| follower | AAL179, CWT category F |
| requirement | 5 NM, a stated wake minimum from TBL 5-5-2 |
| observed | 4.59 NM in trail, deficit 0.41 NM |
| condition | IFR |
| previous snapshot, same pair | 5.39 NM, legal, margin plus 0.39 |

Three things make it different from the residual population the paper explains.

**It violates a stated wake minimum rather than the radar floor.** The
visual-separation explanation covers floor violations: accepted visual
separation voids the radar minima, and 45 of the 46 residual violations in the
corrected window were against the 2.5 NM floor. A category B leader ahead of a
category F follower carries an explicit 5 NM wake minimum, and this pair is
inside it.

**It was measured under instrument conditions**, which is where the
visual-separation explanation stops applying. That is the whole reason the
instrument-condition contrast was worth building.

**The same pair was legal one snapshot earlier**, at 5.39 NM, and compressed to
4.59 NM. Separation compression on final is a documented operational
phenomenon, and the European work on time-based separation and optimised
runway delivery exists partly to manage it. This is the first time this
project has observed a single pair crossing its own requirement between
consecutive snapshots.

## What it is not

It is one pair. A 0.41 NM deficit is larger than ADS-B position error, which
runs to a few hundred feet, but it is not larger than the compound uncertainty
of two smoothed positions and an inferred runway assignment. Both aircraft must
have been correctly attributed to the same final for the pair to mean anything,
and the attribution is the corrected centreline kind rather than the old
heading match, which is better but not proof. The honest description is a
single well-formed observation that the paper's existing explanation does not
cover, in the regime built specifically to test that explanation.

## What would make it evidence

More instrument-condition exposure, which the watcher now accumulates without
help. Roughly thirty instrument-condition pairs would let the violation rate be
compared to the visual one meaningfully. If a stated-wake-minimum violation
under instrument conditions recurs at a rate the visual-separation explanation
cannot absorb, that is a finding about operations rather than about this
detector, and it is the kind a facility data agreement would let someone
qualified check properly.
